## About Spritix

Use [sprite ai](https://spritix.ai) to explore character animation from a full-body reference. The [sprite generator](https://spritix.ai) supports Idle, Walk, Run, Jump, and Attack. Refine timing and loops, then use the [ai sprite sheet generator](https://spritix.ai) to arrange frames. The [sprite sheet maker](https://spritix.ai) exports sprite sheets, GIFs, Atlas JSON, and individual PNG frames.

## Get started

1. Upload a full-body character image or describe a new character.
2. Prepare your character and generate an action.
3. Preview the animation, adjust timing and export settings, and download the files available with your plan.
